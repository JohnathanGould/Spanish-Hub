Who is Milo?

Milo was a real black lab who crashed every Monday morning conference call for years. He had boundless energy, zero filter, and saw only goodness in everyone he met.

The Milo Speaks apps carry his name. Every language, every learner, every feature built — it's all in his memory.

No ads that interrupt. No subscriptions. No paywalls. Learning a language should be free. Milo would have had it no other way.
