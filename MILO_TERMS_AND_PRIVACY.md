## Privacy Policy

### What We Collect
When you create an account, we collect:
- Your display name
- Your email address
- Your profile photo URL (Google Sign-In users only)

When you use the app, we collect:
- Your learning progress (words practiced, correct and incorrect answers, confidence scores)
- Your XP, streaks, and earned badges
- Your daily activity for streak and goal tracking
- Crash reports and error logs (via Sentry) to help us fix bugs
- Anonymous usage data (via PostHog) to understand how the app is used

We do not collect payment information. We do not sell your data. Ever.

### How We Use It
We use your data to:
- Save your learning progress across sessions and devices
- Display your progress on the leaderboard (display name and XP only)
- Improve the app through anonymous analytics and crash reports

### Guest Users
If you use the app without an account, no personal information is collected or stored. Progress made as a guest is not saved between sessions.

### Data Storage
Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, hosted in Montreal, Canada (northamerica-northeast1 region).

### Third-Party Services
Milo Speaks Spanish uses the following third-party services:
- **Google Firebase** — authentication and data storage
- **Sentry** — crash reporting and error monitoring
- **PostHog** — anonymous usage analytics
- **Google Gemini** — powering the Milo AI chat feature

Each service operates under its own privacy policy.
